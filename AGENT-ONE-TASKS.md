## 1. THE CRAZYGAMES SAVE — the priority, and the only thing blocking release

The director's words: *"make the crazy games save a priority"*. The game ships on
crazygames.com. Their SDK stores saves in a key/value data module with a **hard
1 MB limit**, and anything above it is simply not backed up.

**Our save is 16.24 MB.** I have done the exploratory work so you do not have to,
because the director wants this moving. Everything below is measured in a real
browser, not estimated. The long version with the raw figures is in `CODEX.md`
under "MEASURED, 15 August 2026"; this is what you need.

### What the save is made of

```text
TOTAL                        17,038,643      16.24 MB
  senior players             11,224,864      65.9% of the save
  youth academies             4,251,000      484 clubs each keep a full academy,
                                             and only yours is ever looked at
  club metadata                 260,000
  fixtures                      962,895      8,781 rows stored as objects
  freeAgents                    247,024
```

Players are **90%** of the save. Nothing else is close, so nothing else matters.

### Compression is not the answer, and it looks like it should be

```text
raw JSON                     16.24 MB
raw + gzip                    3.67 MB   4.4x
trimmed + gzip                3.34 MB   5.0x
```

`CompressionStream('gzip')` exists in the browser and works. It leaves us **3.3x
over**. Please do not spend a day on field tidying and compression expecting it to
land — it will not, on its own.

### The assumption I got wrong, so you do not inherit it

I originally told you to build a "seed plus diff" format. That rests on the world
being reproducible. **It is not.** Two fresh loads, same club:

```text
run 1   9,890 players   world hash 57fe4fa7
run 2   9,886 players   world hash 13dd8b1c
```

A different *number of players*. There is a seeded `mulberry` PRNG and squads are
built from `mulberry(hashStr(name+'|'+clubIdx))` — which is exactly what misled me
— but there are 317 `Math.random()` calls in the file and enough sit on the
generation path.

### What fits

```text
A  academies dropped, rival senior squads slimmed     gzip 2,246 kB   over by 1,222
B  academies AND rival senior squads regenerated      gzip   136 kB   FITS, 888 kB spare
```

**B is the only one that fits**, and it fits with room for thirty seasons of
accumulated history.

### The job, in order

1. **Make world generation reproducible from one stored seed.** Audit the
   `Math.random()` calls, separate world generation from in-play randomness, put
   generation behind a seeded stream.
   *Acceptance:* generate twice from the same seed and assert identical world
   hashes **including the player count**. Nothing else here can be built until
   this holds, so it is first.
2. **Decide what a rival club is between saves.** Regenerating season one is not
   enough — after five seasons rival squads have diverged through transfers,
   ageing and growth, and that divergence must be stored or recomputed. Two ways,
   and it is your call because it is your lane:
   - store each club's divergence, which grows over a career; or
   - keep rival clubs at reduced fidelity — a squad-strength model plus a handful
     of named men — and **promote a player to full fidelity the moment the manager
     touches him**: scouts him, bids for him, sees him in a match report or a
     scout report. A player he has never heard of does not need to survive a
     reload byte-for-byte; one he has scouted absolutely does.

   I lean to the second. Tell me if you disagree.
3. **The cheap wins**, worth having whichever way you go:
   - `attrs` as a fixed-order array instead of ~19 named keys — 573 bytes a player
     becomes about 60, which is 4.9 MB across the world
   - provenance and flavour text off everyone but your own squad
     (`playerFactsSource`, `playerFactsReadDate`, `playerProfile`, `persNote`,
     `espnId`) — ~293 bytes a player, 2.9 MB across the world
   - fixtures as tuples rather than objects — 940 kB becomes 239 kB
   - `CompressionStream('gzip')` on the way out
4. **A migration path.** Saves in the old shape must keep loading. A career
   somebody is thirty seasons into cannot be thrown away by this.

## 3. THE INJURIES THRESHOLD IS YOURS TO SET

`tests/injuries.test.cjs` fails intermittently — twice in four full runs, on a
different assertion each time:

```text
run A   too many injuries in the first four matches (4)   against run.early <= 3
run B   a 26-man squad picked up 21 injuries in a season  against run.total <= 16
```

I measured four seasons rather than calling it flaky:

```text
mean: 38 league matches, 17.3 cup, 9.0 injuries, 0.5 in the first four
```

The mean is nowhere near the cap — nine against a limit of sixteen — so these are
the tail of a long distribution, not a shifted average. But the tail now reaches
the cap, and I believe the cup calendar fix is why: the club plays about
**seventeen cup matches a season** now, where before most of the League Cup was
unreachable and got settled in one sweep at season end.

More football is correct. More football means more injuries. Two readings and both
are yours: either the caps were fitted to an accidentally short season and should
move, or seventeen cup matches is too many and the entry rounds want revisiting.

**I have not touched your threshold.** A boundary is a decision, not a typo. The
test also wants a seed — an unseeded stochastic assertion over a 55-match season
will keep doing this whatever the bound is.

## 4. THE GROUND AS A LADDER YOU CLIMB

The director wants the climb legible: *"start a really small stadium ... just make
it really good. Everything just makes sense, fun to play."*

Today `CC_CAPS` starts at 1,200 and `pr.type==='rebuild'` multiplies capacity by
1.28 in a single step. Between a bank of grass and a stadium there is nothing.

- **A tier ladder.** A roped-off pitch and a portakabin; one covered terrace; a
  small seated stand; floodlights; a second stand; all-seater; a modern bowl. Each
  with a build cost, a build **duration** — a ground is not rebuilt over a weekend
  — a capacity, and matchday revenue per head that rises with comfort as well as
  with numbers.
- **Make building hurt.** The interesting decision is a stand or a striker, and it
  is only interesting if the stand genuinely tempts.
- **Gates that are not money.** Floodlights before you can be televised; a minimum
  capacity and seating for each division. The real pyramid has ground grading
  rules and they are a wonderful natural obstacle for a climbing club.
- **Promotion into a ground that is too small** is the best version of this
  problem. Please make something of it.

Start lower than we do now — the hardest route should begin at a few hundred.

I want to make the ground visibly grow in the 3D Dugout, so a club that climbs
from the National League can see that it climbed. That needs your tiers to exist
first, which is why this is ahead of the two below.

## 5. THE CHAIRMAN CHOICE IS THE DIFFICULTY SETTING — make it say so

`CC_CHAIRS` already varies budget, bank, wage ceiling, patience and target. That is
a difficulty setting, and the screen never admits it. The director: *"obviously,
they pick an easy route with more money."*

- Widen the spread. The generous route should be a genuinely easier game; the hard
  route should be close to unfair.
- State the trade plainly on the button — "more money now, and the board expects
  promotion in year one" is a difficulty setting a player understands without a
  wiki.
- Make the owner a **relationship that keeps mattering**, not a number applied
  once at creation: what he does after promotion, after relegation, when a bigger
  club comes for you, when he loses interest.

## 6. A CLUB WITH NO PAST SHOULD BE GIVEN ONE

A built club has no history and every screen that might mention one has nowhere to
look. Generate once at creation and store: founded year, nickname, an old best
finish, a former player now on television, and — the good one — **a local rival
chosen from the clubs nearest it**, so there is a derby from the first season.

None of it affects a result. All of it gives the rest of the game something true
to say.

Watch the save budget: this is a few dozen bytes on one club, not a field on ten
thousand players. See task 1.
